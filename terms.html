6. Bank connects

A bank connect is permission to link one new bank account login (one Plaid "Item") to Watch-It.

  • Using a connect. One connect is used the moment a new bank link succeeds. Nothing is used if you close Plaid, the link fails, or you log back in to a bank you've already linked.
  • No refunds for disconnecting. Disconnecting a bank, whether you do it, it happens because your plan ended, or because you deleted your account, doesn't return the connect used to link it.
  • Plan connects reset every 12 months from the day your first paid period started. Unused plan connects don't roll over.
  • Shop connects can be bought in the in-app Shop (currently 1 for $1.99, 3 for $4.99, 5 for $7.49, in US dollars; local prices are shown in Google Play). They're one-time, non-refundable digital purchases except where the law or Google Play's refund policy says otherwise. They don't expire, but using them requires an active Plus or Premium plan. If a Shop purchase is refunded, its connects are removed; if they were already used, your balance can go below zero and the next purchase covers that first.
  • Order of use. Plan connects are used before Shop connects.
  • Bank limit. Your plan limits how many banks can be linked at once, even if you have connects left.
  • When a plan ends. Bank sync pauses. If you don't resubscribe within 7 days of pausing, paused banks are disconnected. Downgrading to a plan with a lower bank limit pauses the extra banks the same way; you can choose which banks to keep. Records already on your phone are never deleted.
  • Connects have no cash value and can't be transferred or exchanged for money.
